# Confirmation of Arrangements for the Cultural Experience

Jack Convery and Hiromi Convery, who conduct tea experiences at Shotoku-An (hereinafter collectively referred to as “Shotoku-An”), and 髙廣信之 (sole proprietor operating under the trade name Awai Studio; hereinafter referred to as “Awai Studio”) mutually confirm and agree to operate the cultural experience held at Shotoku-An in accordance with the following arrangements.

## Article 1 — Experience Details

1. The experience will be held at Shotoku-An.
2. The experience will offer guests an opportunity to experience the “Way of Tea” at Shotoku-An.
3. The experience will include the following:

   - Matcha tea and Japanese sweets
   - The use of traditional tea utensils and charcoal
   - Preparation of the tea-room setting, including traditional attire such as kimono
   - Learning and dialogue through tea

4. The description of the experience published on the Awai Studio website will be based on the content confirmed as of the date of this document. If any changes are required, both parties will confirm them in advance.
5. The experience will last approximately two hours, with sufficient time allowed according to the natural flow of the experience.

## Article 2 — Number of Participants

The experience will be offered to private groups of up to five participants.

## Article 3 — Sales Price

The sales price on the Awai Studio website will be JPY 66,000 per group.

## Article 4 — Payment to Shotoku-An

Awai Studio will close its monthly accounts on the last day of each month and pay Shotoku-An for all experiences held during that month by the fifth day of the following month. If the fifth day falls on a banking holiday, payment will be made by the following business day. Awai Studio will bear all bank transfer fees.

As an initial operating arrangement, Awai Studio will pay Shotoku-An a total of JPY 30,000 for each experience, including the use of Shotoku-An.

This payment includes the following:

- Conducting and guiding the experience at Shotoku-An
- Hiromi’s performance of temae, the formal preparation and serving of tea, during the experience
- The use and preparation of the tea room and any other spaces required for the experience
- Preparation before the experience and cleaning up afterward
- Matcha tea and Japanese sweets
- The use of traditional tea utensils and charcoal
- Preparation of the tea-room setting, including traditional attire such as kimono
- Learning and dialogue through tea

These payment terms are intended for the initial stage of operation. The parties may review and revise them through mutual discussion as necessary, taking into account actual booking conditions, the content of the experience, and the operational burden involved.

As the organizer of the experience, 髙廣信之 will be present at Shotoku-An on the day of each experience to assist the participants and support the operation of the experience.

## Article 5 — Booking Procedure

1. After receiving several proposed dates from a prospective participant, Awai Studio will contact Shotoku-An to confirm availability.
2. As a general rule, the usual available dates and times will be as follows:

   - Monday: 10:00 a.m. or 3:00 p.m.
   - Tuesday: 3:00 p.m.
   - Wednesday: Approximately once every two weeks, subject to confirmation of Shotoku-An’s schedule
   - Thursday: Approximately once every three weeks, subject to confirmation of Shotoku-An’s schedule
   - Friday: 3:00 p.m.
   - Saturday: 10:00 a.m. or 3:00 p.m.

3. Prospective participants will be asked to provide several preferred dates, after which Awai Studio will ask Shotoku-An which dates and times are available.
4. After Shotoku-An confirms an available date and time, Awai Studio will provide the prospective participant with payment instructions.
5. The booking will be confirmed when the participant’s payment has been completed.
6. Once either the 10:00 a.m. or 3:00 p.m. time slot has been booked on a particular day, no booking will be accepted for the other time slot. Only one experience will be held per day.

## Article 6 — Dates on Which Bookings Cannot Be Accepted

If Shotoku-An has specific dates or periods on which bookings cannot be accepted, or plans to be away for an extended period, Shotoku-An will notify Awai Studio in advance whenever reasonably possible.

Awai Studio will reflect this information in its booking arrangements and will make reasonable efforts to ensure that the experience can be operated without placing an excessive burden on Shotoku-An.

## Article 7 — Insurance and Accident Response

1. Awai Studio will obtain or arrange appropriate business liability insurance covering participant injuries, accidents arising from the operation of the experience, accidents arising from the management of the Shotoku-An facilities, and other relevant risks. The specific coverage and scope of compensation will be governed by the terms of the applicable insurance policy.
2. If an accident or other incident occurs, Shotoku-An and Awai Studio will cooperate and take all necessary action, giving the highest priority to the safety of the participants.
3. If an incident falls outside the scope of the insurance coverage, the parties will discuss the appropriate response after considering the cause of the incident, the allocation of responsibility, and the specific circumstances.

## Article 8 — Cancellation by a Guest

1. Cancellations by guests will be handled in accordance with the cancellation policy published on the Awai Studio website.
2. If a guest cancels at least seven days before the scheduled date of the experience and receives a full refund of the amount paid, no payment will be due to Shotoku-An in respect of that booking.
3. If a guest cancels fewer than seven days before the scheduled date of the experience and, under the Awai Studio cancellation policy, does not receive a refund of the amount paid, Awai Studio will pay Shotoku-An the JPY 30,000 specified in Article 4.
4. If a guest receives a full refund under an exception set out in the Awai Studio cancellation policy, including cancellation within 24 hours after payment, no payment will be due to Shotoku-An in respect of that booking.

## Article 9 — Cancellation or Rescheduling After Booking Confirmation

1. If Awai Studio is required to cancel a confirmed experience for reasons attributable to Awai Studio, Awai Studio will bear all payment processing fees, refund fees, insurance arrangement costs, and any other expenses actually incurred by Awai Studio. Awai Studio will also pay Shotoku-An the JPY 30,000 specified in Article 4.
2. If a confirmed experience must be cancelled for reasons attributable to Shotoku-An, Shotoku-An will bear all payment processing fees, refund fees, insurance arrangement costs, and any other expenses actually incurred by Awai Studio. In addition, as compensation for cancelling the confirmed experience, Shotoku-An will pay Awai Studio JPY 19,800, representing 30% of the JPY 66,000 sales price of the experience.
3. The payment processing fees, refund fees, insurance arrangement costs, and other expenses referred to in Paragraphs 1 and 2 will be limited to actual out-of-pocket expenses for which the relevant amounts can be substantiated.
4. Notwithstanding the preceding paragraphs, if the experience must be cancelled due to the sudden or serious illness or injury of Jack Convery, Hiromi Convery, or 髙廣信之, a natural disaster, the issuance of an official warning, the suspension of public transportation, or other unavoidable circumstances not attributable to either party, neither the payment to Shotoku-An under Paragraph 1 nor the compensation payable to Awai Studio under Paragraph 2 will become due. Shotoku-An and Awai Studio will each bear one half of the actual expenses incurred.
5. If cancellation or rescheduling becomes necessary, the affected party will notify the other party promptly after becoming aware of the situation.

## Article 10 — Publication on the Website

After obtaining confirmation from Shotoku-An regarding both the contents of this document and the description of the experience to be published on the website, Awai Studio will publish the experience on the Awai Studio website.

If any material change to the experience description becomes necessary after publication, the parties will confirm the change in advance.

## Article 11 — Publication and Promotional Schedule

Publication and promotion of the experience are planned as follows:

- By the end of August 2026: Publication on the Awai Studio website
- From September 2026 onward: Promotion through social media
- From November 2026 onward: Consideration of whether to list the experience on GetYourGuide. However, GetYourGuide may accept bookings shortly before the scheduled date, and once a booking has been confirmed, the experience is generally expected to be held. Its booking system may therefore be incompatible with the current booking procedure and Shotoku-An’s operating arrangements. Listing on GetYourGuide is not assumed or required and will only be pursued if both parties determine that the experience can be operated without placing an unreasonable burden on Shotoku-An.
- If an application to GetYourGuide is considered, the parties will review GetYourGuide’s listing requirements, fees, booking procedures, cancellation terms, and other relevant conditions after sufficient reviews, photographs, and operational arrangements are in place. The parties will then discuss the matter again. No application will be made if the conditions are not suitable for the operation of the experience.

The actual publication schedule and other plans may be changed depending on the state of preparation and the conditions imposed by the relevant services.

## Article 12 — Review After the Initial Operating Period

The sales price, payment amount, booking procedure, available dates and times, content of the experience, and other operating conditions set out in this document may be changed by mutual agreement after reviewing the initial operation of the experience.

Any changes will not be agreed orally alone. The parties will retain a record of their agreement by email or another method that allows the agreed details to be confirmed.
