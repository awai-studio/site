# Confirmation of Arrangements for the Cultural Experience

Fukujoji Temple (Head Priest: 名流慈頼; hereinafter referred to as “Fukujoji”) and 髙廣信之 (sole proprietor operating under the trade name Awai Studio; hereinafter referred to as “Awai Studio”) mutually confirm and agree to operate the cultural experience held at Fukujoji in accordance with the following arrangements.

## Article 1 — Experience Details

1. The experience will be held at Fukujoji.
2. The experience will include guidance in zazen meditation and dialogue, as well as tea time.
3. The description of the experience published on the Awai Studio website will be based on the content confirmed as of the date of this document. If any changes are required, both parties will confirm them in advance.
4. Each experience will last 90 minutes.

## Article 2 — Number of Participants

1. Each experience will be held for a minimum of three and a maximum of nine participants.
2. As a general rule, the experience will not be held if there are fewer than three participants.

## Article 3 — Sales Price

The sales price on the Awai Studio website will be JPY 16,000 per participant.

## Article 4 — Payment to Fukujoji

Awai Studio will close its monthly accounts on the last day of each month and pay Fukujoji for all experiences held during that month by the fifth day of the following month. If the fifth day falls on a banking holiday, payment will be made by the following business day. Awai Studio will bear all bank transfer fees.

The amount payable by Awai Studio to Fukujoji for each experience will be determined according to the number of participants as follows. The calculation will be based on the number of participants confirmed seven days before the scheduled date of the experience.

These payment terms are intended for the initial stage of operation. The parties may review and revise them through mutual discussion as necessary, taking into account actual booking conditions and the operational burden involved.

## Article 5 — Booking Procedure

1. After receiving proposed dates from a prospective participant, Awai Studio will contact Fukujoji to confirm availability.
2. As a general rule, proposed booking dates may fall on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Thursdays, Fridays, or Saturdays.
3. Prospective participants will be asked to select three proposed dates from among the days listed above.
4. Awai Studio will notify Head Priest Jirai of the proposed dates and ask which dates are available.
5. The date selected by Head Priest Jirai will become the scheduled date of the experience, after which Awai Studio will notify the prospective participant and confirm the booking.

## Article 6 — Starting Times

To make it easier for participants to combine the experience with a visit to Katsura Imperial Villa, the available starting times will be 9:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. Head Priest Jirai will determine whether each time slot is available when the proposed booking dates are being coordinated.

## Article 7 — Meeting Point and Transportation

1. As a general rule, participants will meet and be dismissed at Fukujoji.
2. Participants will arrange their own transportation.
3. Awai Studio may provide general guidance concerning transportation when necessary, but taxi services and other transportation arrangements will not be included in the experience fee.

## Article 8 — Insurance and Accident Response

1. Awai Studio will obtain or arrange appropriate business liability insurance covering participant injuries, accidents arising from the operation of the experience, accidents arising from the management of the Fukujoji facilities, and other relevant risks. The specific coverage and scope of compensation will be governed by the terms of the applicable insurance policy.
2. If an accident or other incident occurs, Fukujoji and Awai Studio will cooperate and take all necessary action, giving the highest priority to the safety of the participants.
3. If an incident falls outside the scope of the insurance coverage, the parties will discuss the appropriate response after considering the cause of the incident, the allocation of responsibility, and the specific circumstances.

## Article 9 — Cancellation by a Guest

1. Cancellations by guests will be handled in accordance with the cancellation policy published on the Awai Studio website.
2. If a guest cancels at least seven days before the scheduled date of the experience and receives a full refund of the amount paid, no payment will be due to Fukujoji in respect of that booking.
3. If a guest cancels fewer than seven days before the scheduled date of the experience and, under the Awai Studio cancellation policy, does not receive a refund of the amount paid, Awai Studio will pay Fukujoji the applicable amount specified in Article 4.
4. If a guest receives a full refund under an exception set out in the Awai Studio cancellation policy, including cancellation within 24 hours after payment, no payment will be due to Fukujoji in respect of that booking.

## Article 10 — Cancellation or Rescheduling After Booking Confirmation

1. If Awai Studio is required to cancel a confirmed experience for reasons attributable to Awai Studio, Awai Studio will bear all payment processing fees, refund fees, insurance arrangement costs, and any other expenses actually incurred by Awai Studio. Awai Studio will also pay Fukujoji the amount specified in Article 4, calculated according to the number of participants booked at the time the decision to cancel is made.
2. If a confirmed experience must be cancelled for reasons attributable to Fukujoji, Fukujoji will bear all payment processing fees, refund fees, insurance arrangement costs, and any other expenses actually incurred by Awai Studio. In addition, as compensation for the cancellation of the confirmed experience, Fukujoji will pay Awai Studio an amount equal to 30% of the total sales price of the experience, calculated according to the number of participants booked at the time the decision to cancel is made.
3. The payment processing fees, refund fees, insurance arrangement costs, and other expenses referred to in the preceding two paragraphs will be limited to actual out-of-pocket expenses for which the relevant amounts can be substantiated.
4. Notwithstanding the preceding paragraphs, if the experience must be cancelled due to the sudden or serious illness or injury of Head Priest Jirai or 髙廣信之, a natural disaster, the issuance of an official warning, the suspension of public transportation, or other unavoidable circumstances not attributable to either party, neither the payment to Fukujoji under Paragraph 1 nor the compensation payable to Awai Studio under Paragraph 2 will become due. Fukujoji and Awai Studio will each bear one half of the actual expenses incurred.
5. If cancellation or rescheduling becomes necessary, the affected party will notify the other party promptly after becoming aware of the situation.

## Article 11 — Publication on the Website

After obtaining confirmation from Fukujoji regarding both the contents of this document and the description of the experience to be published on the website, Awai Studio will publish the experience on the Awai Studio website.

If any material change to the experience description becomes necessary after publication, both parties will confirm the change in advance.

## Article 12 — Publication and Promotional Schedule

Publication and promotion of the experience are planned as follows:

- By the end of August 2026: Publication on the Awai Studio website
- From September 2026 onward: Promotion through social media
- From November 2026 onward: Consideration of whether to list the experience on GetYourGuide. However, GetYourGuide may accept bookings shortly before the scheduled date, and once a booking has been confirmed, the experience is generally expected to be held. Its booking system may therefore be incompatible with the current booking procedure and Fukujoji’s operating arrangements. Listing on GetYourGuide is not assumed or required and will only be pursued if both parties determine that the experience can be operated without placing an unreasonable burden on Fukujoji.
- If an application to GetYourGuide is considered, the parties will review GetYourGuide’s listing requirements, fees, booking procedures, cancellation terms, and other relevant conditions after sufficient reviews, photographs, and operational arrangements are in place. The parties will then discuss the matter again. No application will be made if the conditions are not suitable for the operation of the experience.

The actual publication schedule and other plans may be changed depending on the state of preparation and the conditions imposed by the relevant services.

## Article 13 — Review After the Initial Operating Period

The sales price, payments, booking procedure, available dates and times, and other operating conditions set out in this document may be changed by mutual agreement after reviewing the initial operation of the experience.

Any changes will not be agreed orally alone. The parties will retain a record of their agreement by email or another method that allows the agreed details to be confirmed.

## Article 14 — Consultation

If any matter is not addressed in this document, or if any question arises concerning its interpretation, Fukujoji and Awai Studio will discuss the matter in good faith and seek a mutually acceptable resolution.

As evidence of their confirmation of the above arrangements, the parties will prepare two copies of this document. Fukujoji and Awai Studio will each sign or affix their seal to both copies and retain one copy.
