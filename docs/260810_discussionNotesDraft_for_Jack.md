# **3. Business Liability Insurance and Risk Management**

Awai Studio will arrange business liability insurance to prepare for participant injuries, accidents, and other incidents that may occur in connection with the operation of the experience, subject to the terms and coverage of the insurance policy.

As the business operator, Awai Studio will assume operational responsibility for the experience and will establish and implement the necessary safety and risk-management measures.

This will include risk management relating to the use of the charcoal fire and hearth, participant safety, and procedures for responding to emergencies.

# **4. Cancellation and Rescheduling**

## **Cancellation by a Guest**

Guest cancellations will be handled in accordance with the cancellation policy published on the Awai Studio website.

If a guest cancels at least seven days before the experience and receives a full refund, no compensation will be payable to Jack and Hiromi for that booking.

If a guest cancels less than seven days before the experience and the guest’s payment is non-refundable, the agreed compensation of ¥30,000 will remain payable.

If a guest is entitled to a full refund under the published cancellation policy, including the applicable 24-hour period following payment, no compensation will be payable to Jack and Hiromi.

## **Cancellation for Reasons Within the Control of the Shotoku-an Side**

If a confirmed experience is cancelled for a reason within the reasonable control of the Shotoku-an side, no compensation will be payable for the cancelled experience.

Awai Studio will offer the guest a full refund or the option to reschedule.

The Shotoku-an side will reimburse Awai Studio for any documented and non-recoverable payment processing fees, refund fees, and insurance premiums paid specifically for that experience.

Awai Studio will not claim lost profit or compensation for its own working time.

## **Cancellation by Awai Studio**

If Awai Studio cancels a confirmed experience for reasons within its reasonable control less than seven days before the scheduled date, the agreed compensation of ¥30,000 will remain payable.

No compensation will be payable if Awai Studio cancels at least seven days before the scheduled date.

Awai Studio will be responsible for any payment processing fees, refund fees, or other direct expenses arising from a cancellation within its reasonable control.

## **Circumstances Beyond Either Party’s Control**

Circumstances beyond either party’s reasonable control include:

- A serious deterioration in Jack’s health, hospitalization, or another unavoidable medical circumstance that makes it impossible for him to host the experience
- A natural disaster
- A severe weather warning
- Suspension of public transportation
- A government restriction
- Another comparable circumstance beyond either party’s reasonable control

If the experience cannot take place because of one of these circumstances, the parties will first seek to reschedule the experience.

If rescheduling is not possible, Awai Studio will refund the guest. No compensation will be payable to Jack and Hiromi for an experience that does not take place.

Any documented and non-recoverable payment processing fees, refund fees, and insurance premiums paid specifically for that experience will be shared equally between Awai Studio and the Shotoku-an side.
